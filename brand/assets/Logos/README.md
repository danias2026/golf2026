The event logo and the three partner logos. Hosts MARJCC and MBJCC go side by side at the top, in one color with no box; GMJF always goes alone at the very bottom (see `PartnerLogoStrip`).

- `marjcc-teal.png`, `mbjcc-teal.png`: the host logos in `lagoon-teal` #006868, for cream and white grounds. Lighter parts are a 38% tint.
- `marjcc-white.png`, `mbjcc-white.png`: the host logos in white, for navy, team colors, photos and the sunset.

- `golf-games-classic-logo.png`: the event logo, 4800 x 3240, transparent outside the badge. Any ground.
- `golf-games-classic-logo.pdf`: the same logo as vector, for print and large signs.
